Cravens House to Sunset Rock

Allow roughly two hours plus photographs for the approximately three-mile out-and-back. Follow Cravens Trail toward Bluff Trail, then the Sunset Rock spur; use the NPS trail map for navigation. Rocky terrain, stairs, and exposed cliff edges.

Runner option: Skip this hike, sleep in, and meet everyone downtown. Cravens House grounds are free; a December interior tour is not promised. Skip the hike if trails are icy or conditions are poor.
